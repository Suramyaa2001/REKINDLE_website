Research

REKINDLE studies how one wildfire leads to other hazards, to the loss of power, water and roads, and to hardship for the households that depend on them.

Working with partners

REKINDLE is co-produced with the people who plan for and live with wildfire risk. Workshops and regular conversations with stakeholders help shape what we study and how we judge what we recommend.

Sharing what we learn

Educational materials, research data and code will be made publicly available so others can build on the work. New papers are listed under Publications.

Education and training

We are creating wildfire-focused educational materials for students from middle school through graduate school. Students at all levels will have opportunities for internships with industry partners and national laboratories.

Get involved

If you are a student, a practitioner or a researcher who would like to work with us, write to the team.
