Research that is shaped by communities and shared with them. Dates will be posted here and in News as they are set.

Workshops

An annual REKINDLE workshop brings together community partners, government agencies, utilities and researchers. The first will be held within the first 6 to 9 months of the project, near our California study regions, so people affected by past fires can take part.

Between workshops, quarterly communities of practice meet online. Year 1 focuses on which risk scenarios matter most and where data is missing, Year 2 on reviewing model assumptions, and Year 3 on testing strategy recommendations against real operating constraints.

The date and location of the first workshop have not been announced.

Education and training

We are creating wildfire-focused educational materials for students from middle school through graduate school. PhD, MS and undergraduate researchers receive structured mentoring across risk management, environmental planning, social science and complex systems, and REKINDLE findings will be brought into UB and partner courses.

Internships

Students will have opportunities for internships with industry partners and national laboratories, on problems scoped to real needs.
